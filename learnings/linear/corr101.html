How r is calculated

  1. Find the means x̄ and ȳ (the center of the cloud).
  2. For each point, multiply the distances from the means: (x−x̄)(y−ȳ). Points above-right or below-left of the center give positive products; points above-left or below-right give negative ones.
  3. Average them to get covariance, then divide by the spreads so the result has no units:

r = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) / √[Σ(x−x̄)² · Σ(y−ȳ)²]

The blue line is the line that best follows the cloud. The closer the points hug it, the closer |r| is to 1; its tilt shows the sign of r.

Reading r

|r|Usual wording
0.9 – 1.0very strong
0.7 – 0.9strong
0.4 – 0.7moderate
0.2 – 0.4weak
0 – 0.2very weak / none

Warnings: r only measures linear relationships — a perfect circle, U-shape or sine wave can have r≈0 yet clearly be related. A single outlier can change r a lot. And correlation ≠ causation.
